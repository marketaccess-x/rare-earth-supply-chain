Global Rare Earth Supply Chain

This tool maps the full rare earth supply chain — from mining and processing to end-use manufacturing — and lets you stress-test it against five policy scenarios. China controls roughly 70% of mining and 90% of processing. The concentration metrics on the right show what that means for specific actors under each scenario.

  1. Select a Policy Scenario in the left panel to see how flows shift
  2. Toggle Demand Structure between 2023 baseline and 2026+ trajectory
  3. Use View Filter to isolate specific parts of the chain
  4. Hover over nodes and flow lines for detailed data
  5. Drag to rotate the globe — scroll to zoom
